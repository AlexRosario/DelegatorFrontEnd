Workstreams

The Wire and The Mill

In design

The Wire is the homepage feed of posts from curated politicians, agencies and reputable organizations. The Mill ranks individual creators that users submit and pay to have checked, and may become its own feed later. AI pulls each claim out of a post and checks it against weighted evidence, and true claims go to a "What's happening in America" wall.

  • The Wire (curated only): members of Congress, federal agencies, and reputable organizations across the spectrum. Never removed. The mock's "Delegator users" tab was dropped.
  • Submitting a creator (decided Nov 23): a user pastes a post link in a homepage section. Before charging, we look up the creator's aggregate rating across all their accounts. A disqualified creator is refused at no charge. A new creator gets a profile created once, with no grade and no intake check, and later submissions add to it.
  • The Mill: once a creator has enough checked claims for a grade, they're ranked on The Mill list. Whether creators can earn a place in a feed will be decided later.
  • Process: follows the scientific method. Claims are tied to the creator's exact words, two AI extraction runs are compared, there's a challenge window, test criteria are written before the evidence search, sources are tiered, review is blind, and verdicts can be reopened.
  • Payment: submitters pay for claim extraction only. Payment never affects a verdict.
  • Platforms: YouTube and Bluesky first (free APIs). Instagram is required, through Meta's Business Discovery API, which needs a Meta app, an Instagram Business account linked to a Facebook Page, business verification and App Review. X is paid, so maybe later.
  • Frontend so far: a mock feed with sample posts, in src/components/FeedComponents/ and src/styles/_feed.scss.
